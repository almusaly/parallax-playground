Controls and tips
  • ← → or A D move the character. Space or ↑ jumps. Flyers use ↑ ↓. P pauses.
  • The Character is a layer. Move it between any two layers to place it in front of or behind your artwork.
  • Set the walking line with the Ground slider in the Character layer, or drag the character on the scene.
  • Layers that leave a gap when they repeat are stretched automatically to tile seamlessly. Change this with each layer's Fit setting.
  • Export settings saves a JSON file. Import it after loading the same PSD or images and the layers are matched by name.
  • Upload a sprite sheet (frames side by side in one row) and it animates automatically.
